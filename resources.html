Reproductive Health Resources

Explore reliable educational materials to learn more about PCOS, Endometriosis, reproductive health, and the importance of seeking appropriate healthcare.

Reliable Health Information

Access to accurate and reliable health information is important for understanding PMOS and Endometriosis. The following resources provide additional information on symptoms, diagnosis, treatment, management, and reproductive health.

World Health Organization (WHO)

The World Health Organization provides evidence-based information on reproductive, menstrual, and hormonal health. Its resources can be used to obtain reliable information about PMOS and related reproductive health concerns.

Website: World Health Organization (WHO)

Endometriosis: WHO Endometriosis Fact Sheet

European Society of Human Reproduction and Embryology (ESHRE)

ESHRE provides evidence-based information and guidelines on Endometriosis, fertility, and reproductive health. Its Endometriosis resources provide additional information on symptoms, diagnosis, treatment, and management.

Website: European Society of Human Reproduction and Embryology (ESHRE)

Patient information: ESHRE Patient Guidelines

International PMOS/PCOS Guideline

The International Guideline provides evidence-based recommendations and information concerning the assessment, diagnosis, management, and long-term health considerations associated with PMOS, previously known as PCOS.

Website: International Evidence-based Guideline for the Assessment and Management of PCOS

National Institutes of Health (NIH)

The National Institutes of Health provides health information and research-related resources that support understanding of medical conditions, including Endometriosis.

Website: NIH Endometriosis Information

U.S. Department of Health and Human Services

The Office on Women's Health provides educational information about reproductive health conditions, including Endometriosis and PCOS. These resources explain symptoms, diagnosis, treatment, and related health concerns.

Endometriosis: Office on Women's Health – Endometriosis

PCOS: Office on Women's Health – PCOS

The Endocrine Society

The Endocrine Society provides professional information about hormonal and endocrine health. Its resources also support understanding of developments in the terminology used for PMOS.

Website: The Endocrine Society

American Society for Reproductive Medicine (ASRM)

The American Society for Reproductive Medicine provides information on reproductive health, fertility, and related medical developments. Its publications can help visitors understand the terminology and clinical considerations associated with reproductive conditions.

Website: American Society for Reproductive Medicine (ASRM)

Further Reading

Additional information may be obtained from recognized medical and reproductive-health organizations, peer-reviewed medical literature, and evidence-based clinical guidelines. Visitors are encouraged to use authoritative sources when seeking further information about reproductive health conditions.

Medical Support

Online educational resources are intended to support health awareness and do not replace professional medical assessment. Individuals experiencing persistent, severe, or concerning symptoms should seek assessment from a qualified healthcare professional.

Important Notice

The resources provided on this website are selected to support health education and awareness. Medical information can change as new evidence becomes available. Information should therefore be checked against current guidance from recognized health authorities and qualified healthcare professionals.
